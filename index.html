About Me

Hey, I'm Klawdia! I'm a Romanian variety streamer and content creator. I love getting lost in story-driven games, surviving Resident Evil, testing interesting demos, and discovering games that make me laugh, panic, or question my decisions.

But The Parlor isn't just about gaming. It's also about reacting to videos, having real conversations, drinking coffee, and sharing memorable (sometimes ridiculous) moments with my community. Pull up a chair — there's always room for one more.

Bring Your Brand Into

Good conversations, memorable moments, and a little bit of chaos.

The Parlor is where gaming, genuine reactions, and conversations come together. I love creating content that feels natural, entertaining, and true to who I am — and I believe collaborations should feel the same way.

Whether you're launching a game, introducing a product, or have a creative idea worth exploring, I'm open to partnerships that fit my content and give my community something enjoyable to experience.

Have a product you'd like me to discover? I'm open to receiving PR packages, unboxing products, and testing new things live on stream.
